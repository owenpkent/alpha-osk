Where the controller lives

Four placements of a game controller in the keyboard window, for NIMBUS_INTEGRATION.md. Dark theme, every option at the same key size. Open this file locally. Decided A, the Controller view, and the pointer stays in the stick's area while a stick is held (2026-11-21).

How to try them

Buttons: left click taps. Right click latches (the blue bar), and a left click lets go. Sticks: click a stick to pick it up, then move the pointer with no button held. Click anywhere to drop it back to centre, or right click to leave it held where it is. Click a held stick to release it. The line under each option shows what the pad is sending.

The button with the dashed outline is the one new control: it switches the controller on and off in every option.

When the pointer leaves the window while a stick is held:

Stay in the area: while a stick is held the pointer cannot go further than full deflection in any direction, so it never leaves the keyboard and never lands on the game. The browser stands in for this by locking the pointer and drawing its own arrow; it shows a notice about Esc, and when you drop the stick the real pointer reappears where you picked it up. In the app it would stay where you dropped it.

A

Controller view Chosen

The letter grid swaps for the pad, and the ABC key swaps back. The window stays the size it is today, and the sticks are the largest of the four. You cannot type and steer at the same moment.
B

Two halves beside the letters

A handheld: left stick and D-pad on one side, right stick and face buttons on the other, letters between. Type and play together, but the window is half as wide again, the sticks are smaller, and Guide, L3 and R3 have no room.
C

One panel, like the numpad

A fourth panel on the right. One stick on screen, switched between left and right by the L and R keys (a latched stick keeps its position while the other is shown). Back, Guide, L3 and R3 would need a second page.
D

A floating window

Its own window, like Symbols and Snippets, placed wherever it suits the game. The keyboard is untouched and can be minimized. Two windows to arrange over a game, and the pad is drawn smaller here to keep the pair from covering it.

How much of the game each one covers

Each window on a 1920 x 1080 screen at today's key size (61 px per key unit, five key rows, no function or number row). Blue is the keyboard, cyan the controller.
