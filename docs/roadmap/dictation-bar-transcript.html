Transcript in the bar, auto-copy, and a History button by the mic. Suggestion bar, Dark theme. Open this file locally. Decided H, the text when done, plus the "Copied" badge; History beside the mic; the clear-context button removed (2026-10-26). Hover a button for its name; only the clock is new.

What changes

Transcript. A normal run already shows its words in the bar, cut from the front so the newest words stay visible. A run that AI cleanup or Write with AI holds back shows only "Listening..." today (your call on 2026-10-24). The same holds when Type As You Speak is off. This change shows those waiting words too, then "Polishing..." while Claude works (cleanup only). The options below differ only in how words that are not typed yet look.

History button (dashed outline, new): a clock beside the mic. It opens Settings straight on Dictation, scrolled to History. It appears whenever the mic does. Switch scanners reach it at the next free index (aosk.v1.bar.0.5), and it has a title-bar copy for when suggestions are off.

Auto-copy: when a run ends, its whole text goes on the clipboard, so dictation that landed in the wrong window is one Ctrl+V away. That is the cleaned text after AI cleanup and the raw words if cleanup fails. A Write with AI draft is not auto-copied, since its preview already has Copy. Auto-copy is on by default, with a switch in Settings, Dictation.

How words that are not typed yet look

Each style applies only while the words wait (the box above is ticked). Words that are already typed show as plain text in every option, except F, which works on every run. Untick the box to compare.

A

Plain

Like a normal run. One look for everything.
B

Dimmed, "Draft" tag

Grey italic behind a small tag. The clearest "not final" signal, but the hardest to read.
C

Accent colour

Full-strength text in the theme accent, matching the lit mic. Easy to read, but colour is the only cue.
D

Dotted underline

Plain text with a dotted underline, the way Windows marks text you are still composing (Chinese, Japanese input).
E

Dashed box

Plain text inside a dashed pill-shaped outline: "held here, not sent". Easy to read, and the box is visible from the side of your eye.
F

Pencil icon

Plain text after a small pencil, meaning "still being written". The cue sits in one place at the start of the line.
G

Settled words plain, the words still being heard grey (every run)

The speech service sends rough words first, then settled ones. G shows the rough words in grey until they settle. It shows what is still changing as you speak, not whether the words are typed yet. Can be combined with C, D, E or F.

Or: no live preview

These skip the styles above. The words show (if at all) only once the run has ended, already typed and already on the clipboard.

H

Show the text when done

While you speak: "Listening..." and the level bars, as today. When the run ends, the finished text sits where the pills are, next to the "Copied" badge, until your next key or 8 seconds. You check it once, after it is final.
I

Badge only

No text ever. "Listening..." while you speak, then the "Copied" badge, then the pills.
J

Live, and kept when done

The words show live as you speak (plain here; any style above), and the finished text stays after the run, as in H.

When the run ends Decided

A green "Copied" badge on the bar for 1.5 seconds, as in every bar above (2026-10-26).
