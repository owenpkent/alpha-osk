Why the current ring is invisible

The ring in Main.qml grows by 10 × level pixels and sits at 0.15 + 0.5 × level opacity, where level is the raw peak amplitude. Normal speech peaks between 0.05 and 0.3 of full scale, so the ring moves about 1 px and stays almost transparent. Every option below first maps the raw peak onto a 0 to 1 scale between the silence floor (0.015, the same constant the auto-stop uses) and half scale, in decibels. The two meters show the difference; drag the gain to see that the shown level stays usable from a quiet mic to a loud one.
